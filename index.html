      How scoring works

      Each lead is scored 0–100 on five factors, then classified Hot (80+), Warm (50–79), or Cold (<50).

      • Fit (30%) — industry, Rio location, company size, budget
      • Value (25%) — deal size and repeat-client potential
      • Urgency (20%) — recent triggers and project timeline
      • Relationship (15%) — referrals, engagement, past client
      • Architect channel (10%) — linked architect partners
